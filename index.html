Anatolian Folk Channel Manager

A private, internal tool used by an independent music creator to manage his own YouTube music channels.

What it is

Anatolian Folk Channel Manager is a small application that runs only on the owner's personal computer. It is not a public service: it has exactly one user (the channel owner), it is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone, and it only accesses channels owned by that user.

What it does

  • Adds localized titles and descriptions (English, German, Russian, Azerbaijani, Arabic, Persian) to the owner's own videos.
  • Optimizes titles, descriptions and tags of the owner's own videos, keeping a backup of the previous version before every change.
  • Uploads and schedules the owner's own original music videos and Shorts, and sets their thumbnails.
  • Replies to thank-you and praise comments on the owner's own videos.
  • Organizes the owner's own playlists.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics for the owner's own channels to measure the results of these changes.
